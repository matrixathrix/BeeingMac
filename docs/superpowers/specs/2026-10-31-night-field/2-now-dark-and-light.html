What carries over between the two

  • Violet, not pink. Soft: #DCCDF6 → #F8F5FD. Deep: #CDB6F2 → #F3EEFC. The text ink shifts with it to a violet-black, #24123A. The glow sits at the top, as in dark.
  • Glass inverts. In dark, shapes are white at 7% with white text. In light, they're white at 70% with aubergine ink, so they read as frosted panes either way.
  • Honey keeps its job. The Save fill is the same #F5A623 in both. Honey text in light uses the deeper #9A6000 for contrast, as the app already does.
  • Score colours are untouched. Only the chosen cell and the core use them, plus the today strip.
