A · NIGHT FIELD

One surface, no seams

The aubergine stops being a header and becomes the whole tab: a night sky with a soft glow at the top. With no band edge and no card edge, the band, the card and the button can't look like three apps any more.

  • Glass shapes only (white at 7%), and one radius family: circles and pills.
  • The dial cells are frosted glass. The chosen one floods with its score colour and so does the core.
  • The one honey thing on screen is the Save button, which honey is for anyway.
  • In the caught-up state the same sky holds a big closed ring, and the bee flies in it.

Trade-off: the Now tab is always dark, even in light mode. That's bold and very on-brand, but it's a deliberate break from the calm-page rule.

B · PAPER & INK

Calm page, one purple object

The band shrinks into a single purple tile, the "hour ticket", that sits on the page like any other card. That puts it in the same family as everything around it. The page is calm, and the ticket is the one jewel.

  • The dial becomes one ring of ten arc segments, tinted red, amber and green with low to high going round. It reads as a scale, not a keypad. The chosen segment glows and the core shows the number.
  • Small labels ("WHAT WERE YOU DOING?") give the screen a reading order.
  • Tags are outlined and left-aligned, so they're quieter than the dial.
  • The caught-up state reuses the ticket as "All caught up", with the same cards below it.

Trade-off: it's the most restrained of the three and fits the Past and Settings look best, but it's the least dramatic.

C · STAGE & SHEET

The dial goes on stage

Two zones, each with one job. The purple stage holds the question and the dial, the moment itself. A sheet rises from the bottom holding everything that follows: tags, note and Save. The sheet's grabber and curved top say "this is attached", so the two zones look made for each other.

  • The dial lives in the purple as white arc segments. Only the chosen one takes its score colour.
  • The countdown moves into the sheet header, next to the action it's counting down to.
  • In the caught-up state the stage holds the closed ring and the sheet holds today's strip and the bee.

Trade-off: the dial gets less height, and expanding the tags needs the sheet to grow over the stage. It's a known pattern, but it's motion work.
