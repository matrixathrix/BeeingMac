Streak tab · History, collapsed and expanded

Each phone is scrolled to the bottom of the Streak tab. Collapsed (the default), History is one glass panel showing a peek at the latest event, so it never looks empty. Expanded, it opens in place into rows grouped by week. Each row has a round tile in the event's colour, a title with its detail, and the time on the right.

How History behaves

  • Collapsed: the title, a one-line peek at the latest event ("Super day · 30 Oct") and a chevron. The whole panel is the tap target.
  • Expanded: it opens in place, with the chevron flipped. Rows are grouped under small headers (This week, Earlier in October, September) instead of one long divider list. It still covers the last 3 months.
  • Row tiles carry the meaning by colour and icon together: honey hexagon = day complete, gold spark = Super day, honey shield = saver used, faint clock = past hour added, red = streak broken. A Super day that earned a saver stays one row, as today.
  • The time sits on the right in small type, so titles get the full width.
